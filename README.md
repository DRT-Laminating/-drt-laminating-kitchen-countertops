## 🪵 Hardwood vs Laminate Countertops

Two options that are often considered for kitchens are hardwood and laminate.

### Hardwood Kitchen Countertops

Hardwood offers the natural character of timber.

The grain, colour variations and texture of real wood can give a kitchen a warm and distinctive appearance.

Wooden kitchen countertops can work particularly well with natural, farmhouse, classic and contemporary kitchen designs.

However, hardwood can require more care depending on the timber and finish. Appropriate maintenance may be necessary to help protect the surface from moisture, stains and everyday wear.

### Laminate Kitchen Countertops

Laminate offers a practical alternative with a wide range of colours, patterns and decorative finishes.

It can provide the appearance of wood and other decorative materials while offering a practical and cost-effective surface for many kitchen projects.

Laminate is also generally straightforward to maintain, making it an option worth considering for busy kitchens.

## 🎨 Choosing the Right Countertop Profile

The edge profile of your countertop can have a noticeable effect on the overall appearance of your kitchen.

### Bullnose (Delta) Postform Tops

Bullnose or delta profiles provide a softer, more rounded countertop edge.

This can work well when you want a more traditional or softer appearance.

### Squareline Postform Tops

Squareline profiles provide a more defined and contemporary appearance.

They can work particularly well with modern cabinetry and clean kitchen designs.

### Flat-Pressed Countertops

Flat-pressed countertops provide another option for projects where a non-postformed surface is preferred.

The right profile depends on your kitchen design, cabinetry and the appearance you want to achieve.

## 🧽 Think About Maintenance and Everyday Use

Kitchen countertops are exposed to spills, food preparation, moisture and regular cleaning.

The way you use your kitchen should therefore influence your countertop choice.

Laminate can be attractive for homeowners looking for a practical surface that is relatively straightforward to maintain.

Hardwood can provide a natural and distinctive appearance, but may require more attention depending on the timber and finish.

Regardless of the countertop material, good care and proper installation are important for maintaining the surface over time.

Avoid exposing countertop surfaces to excessive heat, standing water or unnecessary damage, and follow the appropriate care recommendations for the selected material.
